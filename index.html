ABOUT AIRVISTA FLIGHT ACADEMY

Safety, knowledge and professional excellence

02 / 12

Building the Future of Aviation Through Safety, Knowledge and Professional Excellence

AIRVISTA FLIGHT ACADEMY is an international aviation education initiative being developed with the vision of delivering professional, accessible and high-quality aviation training in a modern European environment.

With a strong focus on safety, operational excellence and professional development, AIRVISTA aims to create a comprehensive training platform for aspiring pilots, aviation professionals and individuals seeking to build a career in the global aviation industry.

Based around Lelystad Airport in the Netherlands, AIRVISTA is designed to connect international aviation students with European training opportunities, professional standards and a learning environment that combines theoretical knowledge with practical experience.

OUR VISION

Our vision is to become a recognised international aviation training organisation, bringing together knowledge, experience and modern training methods to prepare the next generation of aviation professionals.

We believe that aviation education should go beyond obtaining a licence or completing a course. It should develop sound judgement, technical competence, professional discipline and a strong safety culture.

OUR MISSION

Our mission is to support students and aviation professionals through structured training pathways, qualified instruction and a professional learning environment.

We aim to:

Promote the highest standards of aviation safety and professionalism.

Provide structured aviation education and career development pathways.

Connect international students with European aviation training opportunities.

Encourage practical skills, technical knowledge and operational awareness.

Support students throughout their training journey, from initial guidance to professional development.

OUR TRAINING APPROACH

AIRVISTA aims to develop a flexible international training model that combines theoretical education, practical flight training, simulator-based learning and specialised aviation courses.

Depending on the programme, regulatory requirements and approved training arrangements, theoretical instruction may be delivered internationally, while practical training and specialised activities may be conducted in Europe.

This approach is intended to make professional aviation education more accessible to students from different countries while maintaining a strong commitment to quality and safety.

OUR COMMITMENT

At AIRVISTA, safety is not simply a training subject; it is the foundation of our philosophy.

We are committed to promoting a culture of responsibility, continuous learning, professional integrity and operational excellence.

Our goal is to help shape confident, competent and safety-conscious aviation professionals who are prepared to meet the demands of an evolving global aviation industry.

AIRVISTA FLIGHT ACADEMY Safety First. Professionalism Always.

LELYSTAD AIRPORT – THE NETHERLANDS

A Professional Environment for Aviation Training

Located in the heart of the Netherlands, Lelystad Airport is one of the country's leading General Aviation airports and an established centre for flight training and aviation activities.

With a long-standing aviation history, a controlled airspace environment and a diverse General Aviation community, Lelystad Airport offers an excellent setting for developing practical flying skills and professional aviation knowledge.

The airport is home to flight training organisations, aircraft operators, aviation service providers and other specialist companies. Its active aviation environment provides students with valuable opportunities to become familiar with real-world airport operations.

WHY LELYSTAD AIRPORT?

Controlled Airport Environment: Experience professional air traffic control procedures and radio communication with Air Traffic Control.

Flight Training Environment: An established location for private and professional pilot training.

Modern Runway Infrastructure: A runway approximately 2,700 metres long and 45 metres wide.

Aviation Community: Access to a diverse network of aviation companies and specialist services.

Strategic Location: Conveniently located in the Netherlands, with access to road and public transport connections.

Practical Learning Opportunities: A real operational environment in which students can develop situational awareness, communication skills and flight discipline.

THE RIGHT AIRCRAFT FOR EVERY STAGE OF FLIGHT TRAINING

At AIRVISTA FLIGHT ACADEMY, our planned training fleet is designed to support students throughout their aviation learning journey, from their first flight to advanced professional pilot training.

Our fleet concept is centred on the renowned Piper aircraft family, combining reliable single-engine trainers with dedicated multi-engine training aircraft.

PIPER PA-28 WARRIOR

Foundation Flight Training

A widely recognised single-engine training aircraft, designed to help student pilots develop essential flying skills.

Key features:

Single-engine piston aircraft

Four-seat cabin

Fixed landing gear

Conventional flight controls

Suitable avionics for basic flight training

Ideal platform for initial pilot development

Training focus: Basic Flight Training, PPL(A), Circuit Training, Solo Flights and Navigation.

PIPER PA-28 ARCHER

Advanced Single-Engine Training

A versatile and capable single-engine aircraft suitable for structured flight training and cross-country operations.

Key features of the modern Archer TX configuration:

180 HP Lycoming engine

Four-seat cabin

Garmin G1000 NXi glass cockpit

Integrated flight and navigation displays

Modern communication and navigation systems

Fixed landing gear

Training focus: PPL(A), CPL(A), Cross-Country Navigation, Advanced VFR and SEP training.

PIPER PA-44 SEMINOLE

Multi-Engine Flight Training

A dedicated twin-engine aircraft designed to prepare pilots for multi-engine operations and advanced flight procedures.

Key features of the modern Seminole configuration:

Two 180 HP Lycoming engines

Four-seat cabin

Constant-speed, full-feathering propellers

Retractable landing gear

Garmin G1000 NXi avionics

Advanced flight and navigation displays

Training focus: MEP Class Rating, Multi-Engine Procedures, Engine Failure Management and Asymmetric Flight Training.

OUR TRAINING PHILOSOPHY

Each aircraft has a specific role within our planned training structure.

From fundamental aircraft handling to advanced multi-engine operations, our objective is to provide a progressive learning experience supported by professional instruction, modern training methods and a strong safety culture.

Training Outcome:

Successful completion of the applicable training, assessments and skill test, subject to regulatory requirements and the relevant ATO approval.

BOEING 737 NG TYPE RATING

Professional Training for Boeing Next Generation Aircraft

The BOEING 737 NG Type Rating Program is designed to provide qualified pilots with the technical knowledge and operational skills necessary to operate the Boeing 737 Next Generation aircraft.

The training program focuses on aircraft systems, flight deck procedures, automation management, operational performance, and the practical application of normal and non-normal procedures.

Training Subjects:

Boeing 737 NG Aircraft Systems

Flight Deck Familiarization and Instrumentation

Flight Management Computer (FMC)

Autoflight and Flight Guidance Systems

Electrical, Hydraulic and Pneumatic Systems

Flight Controls and Aircraft Limitations

Normal, Abnormal and Emergency Procedures

Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

Performance and Flight Planning

Crew Resource Management (CRM)

Full Flight Simulator (FFS) Training

Line Operations and Operational Procedures

Training Objective:

To prepare pilots for safe, efficient, and professional operation of Boeing 737 NG aircraft through structured theoretical and practical training.

Training Outcome:

Successful completion of the required theoretical examinations, simulator training, assessments and skill test, in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements and ATO approval.
