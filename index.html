About the Academy

A vision built around the entire aviation journey.

AIRVISTA Flight Academy brings together the planning, training pathways and student guidance needed to pursue a professional aviation career.

Based around Lelystad Airport in the Netherlands, AIRVISTA's programme concept spans the first flight, licensing, airline type-rating preparation and complementary aviation disciplines. The objective is to connect each stage into one clearly planned route rather than leave students to piece it together alone.

Industry-focused learning

Decision-making, safety culture, standard radio communication, multi-crew coordination and professional habits.

Personal pathway planning

Eligibility review, programme selection, training guidance and next-step career preparation.

The Pilot Journey

Zero experience.
One clear flight path.

A step-by-step overview of the journey from early orientation through professional licensing and airline preparation.

  1. 01
    Orientation

    Eligibility, aptitude, programme selection and personal training plan.

  2. 02
    Private Pilot Licence

    Theory, navigation, radio, first solo and skill test.

  3. 03
    Hour Building

    Build flight time, judgement and practical flying experience.

  4. 04
    Commercial Pilot Licence

    Professional flying and commercial operations skills.

  5. 05
    Instrument Rating

    Instrument flight, IFR procedures and approach operations.

  6. 06
    Multi-Engine Rating

    Twin-engine handling, engine-out and asymmetric operations.

  7. 07
    ATPL Theory

    Structured airline-level theoretical knowledge.

  8. 08
    MCC / CRM

    Multi-crew coordination and crew resource management.

  9. 09
    Type Rating

    Aircraft-specific A320 or Boeing 737NG preparation.

  10. 10
    Airline Preparation

    Interview, CV, simulator assessment and recruitment readiness.

Indicative overall duration: integrated pilot training approximately 12–18 months; ATPL theory approximately 6–9 months. Timing depends on individual progress, availability and aviation-authority requirements. Training components and sequence are subject to eligibility and programme design.

Prepare for the flight deck.

Aircraft-specific Type Rating pathways for eligible pilots, incorporating ground school, simulator training, testing and applicable base-training phases.

Airbus A320 Family

A320 Type Rating

Typical duration: 6–8 weeks. An aircraft-specific programme covering fly-by-wire operation, sidestick philosophy, autoflight systems, procedures and multi-crew work.

  • Ground school and systems training
  • Normal, abnormal and emergency simulator scenarios
  • Skill-test preparation and applicable base training
Boeing 737 Next Generation

B737NG Type Rating

Typical duration: 6–8 weeks. Aircraft-specific training focused on conventional control philosophy, flight management, automation, manual handling and flight-crew coordination.

  • Ground school and aircraft limitations
  • Full-flight simulator training and assessments
  • Skill test and applicable base training
STAGE 01Ground school
STAGE 02Simulator training
STAGE 03Skill test
STAGE 04Base training where applicable

Entry requirements may include commercial licence, ME/IR, ATPL theory, MCC/JOC, valid Class 1 medical and ICAO English proficiency. Final eligibility and certification are determined by the applicable authority and training provider.
